Which injectable smooths wrinkles?

Wrinkle relaxers (neuromodulators) such as Xeomin and Daxxify soften lines that form when you move — the “11s” between your brows, forehead lines and crow’s feet. You still look like you, just more rested.

Wrinkle relaxer or filler: what’s the difference?

Wrinkle relaxers calm the muscles that crease your skin; filler adds volume under it. Many people use both. Here’s how they compare.

Injectables FAQs

What’s the difference between tox and filler?

Tox (a neuromodulator such as Xeomin or Daxxify) relaxes the muscles that cause lines when you move. Filler is hyaluronic acid that adds volume and shape, for example to lips or cheeks. Tox softens movement lines; filler restores what time has taken away. Many plans use both.

What’s the difference between Xeomin and Daxxify?

Both soften expression lines. Xeomin contains only the active ingredient and typically lasts up to 3 months. Daxxify is peptide-formulated and can last 6–9 months for many people, so you visit less often. Your provider will help you choose based on your goals and budget.

How long do injectables last?

It depends on the product: Xeomin up to about 3 months, Daxxify up to 6–9 months, and hyaluronic acid filler about 6–18 months depending on the area. Results vary from person to person.

Do injections hurt?

Most people feel a quick pinch. The needles are very fine, wrinkle-relaxer visits take about 15 minutes, and most fillers contain lidocaine to keep you comfortable. Topical numbing is available if you’d like it.

Is there downtime after injectables?

Usually very little. You can return to work the same day. Mild swelling or bruising at injection sites can happen, especially with filler, and typically fades within a few days.

Can filler be reversed?

Yes. Hyaluronic acid filler can be dissolved with hyaluronidase if you’re unhappy with the result or it needs correcting. GLO offers filler dissolving, including for filler placed elsewhere.
