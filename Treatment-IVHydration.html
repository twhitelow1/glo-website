IV Hydration Therapy

A customized IV drip of fluids, vitamins, and minerals to support hydration, energy, immune function, or recovery — tailored to what your body needs that day.

Benefits

Hydration

Fast fluid replenishment beyond what drinking water alone provides.

Energy

B-vitamin blends support natural energy production.

Immune support

Vitamin C and zinc blends to support your immune system.

Recovery

Popular after travel, illness, workouts, or a big weekend.

What to Expect

A quick health screening confirms IV hydration is right for you, then a licensed provider places a small IV catheter, usually in your arm. Your chosen blend infuses over 30–60 minutes while you relax in a comfortable lounge chair. Most clients feel the effects the same day.

Aftercare

  • Keep the small bandage on the injection site for a few hours.
  • Minor bruising at the site is possible and typically fades within days.
  • No downtime — resume normal activity right away.

FAQ

What's in an IV hydration drip?

A base of sterile fluids plus a customized blend of vitamins, minerals, and electrolytes matched to your goals.

How long does a session take?

Most sessions take 30 to 60 minutes depending on the blend and infusion rate.

Is IV hydration safe?

It's administered by a licensed provider after a brief health screening. Minor risks like bruising at the injection site are possible.
