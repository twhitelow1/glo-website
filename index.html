GLO Med Spa FAQs

What is a med spa?

A med spa (medical spa) pairs a calm spa setting with medical treatments — injectables, lasers, medical-grade skin care and wellness therapies — performed by licensed providers under a physician’s direction. GLO Aesthetics + Wellness Lounge is a med spa in Ocala and Palatka, FL.

Where is GLO Aesthetics + Wellness Lounge?

GLO has two Florida locations: 1925 SW 18th Ct, Unit 109, Ocala, FL 34471, and 210 St Johns Ave, Palatka, FL 32177. Call 352-559-8034 or see both locations.

What treatments does GLO offer?

Injectables (Xeomin, Daxxify, dermal and lip filler, liquid rhinoplasty), skin services (Tetra CO2 and Motus laser, CoolPeel, laser hair removal, Everesse radiofrequency, Radiant Lift (Everesse + CoolPeel), facials, chemical peels and SkinPen microneedling) and medical wellness (weight loss, hormone therapy, IV hydration and peptides). Compare all treatments.

Do I need a consultation before my first treatment?

Yes. Every new client starts with a consultation, so a licensed provider can review your goals and health history and recommend a plan. There’s never pressure to book anything during the visit.

What are GLO’s hours?

The Ocala location is open Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 9am–5pm, Wednesday 9am–6pm, and weekends by appointment. Palatka hours vary; see real-time availability on our online booking calendar or call 352-559-8034.

Do you offer memberships or financing?

Yes. The GLO Club membership includes a Signature Facial every month, 10% off all treatments and skincare, and priority booking. Financing through Cherry lets you pay over time. Learn about financing.
