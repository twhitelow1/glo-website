Common Questions

Is laser skin revitalization FDA-cleared?
Yes. The laser platforms we use — Tetra CO2, CoolPeel, and Motus — hold FDA 510(k) clearance for skin resurfacing, soft-tissue coagulation, and treatment of fine lines, textural irregularities, and pigmented lesions.
What's the difference between Tetra CO2, CoolPeel, and Motus?
Tetra CO2 is our most powerful option for significant sun damage, scarring, and deeper wrinkles (5–7 days downtime). CoolPeel delivers similar CO2 technology with faster, lower downtime. Motus Laser Facial is our gentlest, virtually no-downtime option for regular maintenance. We'll help you choose at your consultation.
How much downtime should I expect?
Downtime depends on the platform: little to none with Motus, a few days with CoolPeel, and typically 5–7 days of redness and peeling with Tetra CO2's standard fractional settings.
Am I a candidate if I have darker skin?
We assess your Fitzpatrick skin type and adjust settings accordingly at your consultation — darker skin tones require more conservative settings to minimize pigment-change risk.
