Common Questions

Is hormone replacement therapy FDA-approved?
Testosterone and bioidentical hormone products are FDA-approved prescription medications for diagnosed hormone deficiency. We confirm your candidacy with lab work before starting any protocol.
How is bioidentical different from synthetic hormones?
Bioidentical hormones are molecularly identical to what your body naturally produces. We'll discuss FDA-approved and compounded options and their differences at your consultation.
How soon will I feel a difference?
Many clients notice improved energy and mood within 2–4 weeks, with fuller results — sleep, body composition, mental clarity — developing over 3–6 months.
