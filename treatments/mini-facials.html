Common Questions

How is this different from a full facial?
A mini facial skips the extended massage and multi-step masking of a full facial to fit a shorter appointment — great for maintenance rather than treating a specific concern.
Can I wear makeup right after?
Yes — there's no downtime, so you can return to your day immediately.
How often should I book one?
Monthly is popular for maintenance, or anytime you want a quick refresh before an event.
