How does CoolPeel work?

CoolPeel is a protocol on the Tetra CO2 laser. It delivers very brief, low-density pulses of CO2 laser energy that treat only the outermost layer of your skin. That controlled, shallow treatment prompts your skin to shed damaged surface cells and make fresh collagen. Deeper layers are spared, so recovery takes days rather than a week or more. Because each session is superficial, improvements build over a series.

Is CoolPeel right for me?

A good fit if…

  • ✓You'd like to soften fine lines, sun spots or dullness
  • ✓You want CO2 laser results with only a few days of redness
  • ✓You have mild acne scarring or enlarged pores
  • ✓You can commit to a series of 3 or more sessions about a month apart

Talk to us first if…

  • –You're pregnant or breastfeeding

  • –You have deeper wrinkles or significant scarring — Tetra CO2 laser skin revitalization is usually the better fit

CoolPeel FAQs

How is CoolPeel different from standard CO2 resurfacing?

CoolPeel uses very brief, low-density laser passes that treat only the outermost skin layer, which is why downtime is measured in days instead of a week or more.

Can I go back to normal activity the same day after CoolPeel?

Most clients return to normal activity and makeup within 24–72 hours, much faster than traditional fractional CO2 resurfacing.

Is one CoolPeel session enough?

Because it's superficial, most clients do a series of 3 or more sessions spaced about a month apart for the best cumulative results.

Does CoolPeel hurt?

CoolPeel is usually very tolerable. We apply a numbing cream first, and most people feel only warmth and a light prickling during the quick laser passes. Your skin may feel warm, like a mild sunburn, for a few hours afterward.

What should I avoid after CoolPeel?

Avoid direct sun and wear sunscreen every day while your skin recovers. Skip retinoids, exfoliants and harsh products until your provider says it's okay. Don't pick at any flaking skin. Most people are back to makeup within 24–72 hours.

CoolPeel vs. Tetra CO2: which is right for me?

CoolPeel suits you if you want a refresh with 24–72 hours of downtime and can do a series. Tetra CO2 is stronger and better for significant sun damage, deeper wrinkles and acne scarring, with 5–7 days of downtime. We'll help you choose at your consultation.
