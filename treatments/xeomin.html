What does Xeomin treat?

Xeomin treats lines caused by facial movement — the creases that show when you frown, squint or raise your brows, and that start to stay put over time.

Glabellar Lines (“11s”)

The vertical frown lines between the brows.

Forehead Lines

Horizontal lines from raised-brow expressions.

Crow's Feet

Fine lines fanning from the outer eye corners.

Expression Wrinkles

Dynamic lines caused by repeated muscle movement.

How does Xeomin work?

Xeomin (incobotulinumtoxinA) temporarily blocks the nerve signals that tell a small muscle to contract. With the muscle relaxed, the skin above it stops folding, so existing lines soften and new ones are slower to form. Unlike some other options it contains no accessory proteins, only the active ingredient. The effect wears off gradually as nerve signalling returns.

Is Xeomin right for me?

A good fit if…

  • ✓You notice “11s” between your brows, forehead lines or crow's feet when you move your face
  • ✓You want to look rested and like yourself, not frozen
  • ✓You'd like a quick treatment with no real downtime
  • ✓You'd prefer a formula without accessory proteins

Talk to us first if…

  • –You're pregnant or breastfeeding
  • –You have a neuromuscular condition such as myasthenia gravis
  • –You've had an allergic reaction to a botulinum toxin product
  • –Your main concern is lost volume or deep folds at rest — dermal filler is usually the better fit

Xeomin FAQs

How is Xeomin different from other neuromodulators?

Xeomin is a “naked” formula with no accessory complexing proteins, which Merz (its manufacturer) cites as a reason for a lower theoretical risk of your body building resistance to treatment over time.

How long does Xeomin last?

Most clients see visible smoothing for up to 3 months, with results typically appearing within about a week of treatment.

Is Xeomin FDA-approved?

Yes. Xeomin (incobotulinumtoxinA) is FDA-approved for moderate-to-severe glabellar lines, with an additional approval covering forehead lines and crow's feet treated at the same time.

Do Xeomin injections hurt?

Most clients describe it as a quick pinch — the needle is tiny and each injection takes only a second. We can also apply a topical numbing cream beforehand if you'd like.

Who shouldn't get Xeomin?

Xeomin isn't recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding, or for clients with certain neuromuscular conditions or known allergies to botulinum toxin products. We'll review your health history at your Aesthetics Consultation to confirm you're a good candidate.

What should I avoid after Xeomin?

For the rest of the day, skip strenuous exercise, avoid rubbing or massaging the treated areas, and stay upright for about 4 hours. You can return to work and normal routines right away.

What's the difference between Xeomin and Daxxify?

Both relax expression lines. Xeomin typically lasts up to 3 months and contains only the active ingredient; Daxxify is peptide-formulated and can last 6 months or more for many people. Your provider will help you choose at your consultation.
