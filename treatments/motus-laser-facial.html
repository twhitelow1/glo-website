How does the Motus Laser Facial work?

The Motus Laser Facial uses the DEKA Motus platform and its in-motion technology. Instead of firing high-intensity spots, your provider glides the handpiece continuously over your skin while the laser energy builds gradually to a comfortable level. This spread-out delivery gently heats the skin to target uneven pigment and redness and support collagen without wounding the surface. Because it's gentle, improvements build over a series of monthly sessions.

Is the Motus Laser Facial right for me?

A good fit if…

  • ✓You have uneven tone, mild redness or early sun damage
  • ✓You have sensitive skin or you're new to laser treatments
  • ✓You want a glow with no downtime and little to no discomfort
  • ✓You'd like regular monthly maintenance for tone and texture

Talk to us first if…

  • –You're pregnant or breastfeeding
  • –You have an active cold sore, skin infection or open wound in the treatment area
  • –You have a fresh sunburn or tan — wait until it fades
  • –You want to treat deeper wrinkles, acne scars or significant sun damage — CoolPeel or Tetra CO2 laser skin revitalization is usually the better fit

Motus Laser Facial FAQs

Does the Motus laser facial hurt?

No — it's designed to be virtually painless thanks to its in-motion delivery technology, which spreads energy gradually rather than firing high-intensity spots.

How is the Motus Laser Facial different from CO2 resurfacing?

It's a much gentler, no-downtime treatment focused on gradual tone and texture improvement, rather than the more dramatic (and more intense) results of ablative CO2 resurfacing.

How many Motus Laser Facial sessions do I need?

Because it works gradually, a series of monthly sessions delivers the best cumulative results.

What should I avoid after a Motus Laser Facial?

There's no downtime, so you can go back to your day right away. For a few days, protect your skin from the sun, wear sunscreen daily and go easy on exfoliants or retinoids if your skin feels sensitive. Your provider will give you aftercare tips.

Is the Motus Laser Facial safe for darker skin?

We assess your skin type at your consultation and adjust the settings to match. Darker skin tones call for more conservative settings to lower the risk of pigment changes. We'll let you know whether the Motus Laser Facial is a good fit for you.

Motus Laser Facial vs. CoolPeel: which should I choose?

The Motus Laser Facial is our gentlest option, with no downtime and gradual results over monthly sessions. CoolPeel uses CO2 laser technology for more noticeable resurfacing, with 24–72 hours of redness. We'll help you choose based on your skin and schedule.
