Treatment Questions, Answered

What treatments does GLO Aesthetics + Wellness Lounge offer?

GLO offers three groups of treatments in Ocala and Palatka, FL: injectables (Xeomin, Daxxify, dermal and lip filler, liquid rhinoplasty and filler dissolving), skin services (CO2 laser resurfacing, CoolPeel, Motus laser facials, laser hair removal, Everesse radiofrequency tightening, Radiant Lift (Everesse + CoolPeel), custom facials, chemical peels and SkinPen microneedling) and medical wellness (weight loss, hormone replacement therapy, IV hydration and peptide therapy).

How do I know which treatment is right for me?

Start with a consultation. A licensed provider looks at your skin, health history and goals, explains your options — including downtime and cost — and recommends a plan. You never have to commit to anything during the visit.

Do I need a consultation before my first treatment?

Yes. Every new client starts with a consultation so your plan fits your goals and health history. Some services, such as Mini Facials and custom facials, can also be booked directly once you’re established.

Which treatments have little or no downtime?

Xeomin, Daxxify, Motus laser facials, Everesse skin tightening, laser hair removal, Mini Facials and IV hydration typically have little to no downtime. CO2 laser resurfacing has the most, usually 5–7 days. Your provider will confirm what to expect for you.

Do you offer memberships or financing?

Yes. The GLO Club and our wellness memberships include monthly treatments and member pricing, and financing through Cherry lets you spread the cost into monthly payments.

Where are GLO’s locations?

GLO has two Florida locations: 1925 SW 18th Ct, Unit 109, Ocala, FL 34471, and 210 St Johns Ave, Palatka, FL 32177. Both offer the full treatment menu. Call 352-559-8034 or book online.

Not sure where to start?

Tell us what you’d like to feel again. We’ll listen first, then walk you through your options — no pressure.
