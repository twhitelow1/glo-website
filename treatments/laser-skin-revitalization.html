How does laser skin revitalization work?

Our most powerful option, Tetra CO2, is a fractional CO2 laser. It creates tiny columns of controlled heat in the skin while leaving the surrounding skin untouched, which helps you heal faster. Your body replaces the treated skin with fresh skin and builds new collagen for 3–6 months. CoolPeel uses the same CO2 technology with brief, shallow pulses for less downtime, and the DEKA Motus laser facial is our gentlest, no-downtime option.

Is laser skin revitalization right for me?

A good fit if…

  • ✓You have sun damage, age spots or uneven tone you'd like to fade
  • ✓You're bothered by fine lines, deeper wrinkles or acne scarring
  • ✓You want smoother texture and smaller-looking pores
  • ✓You can plan around some downtime, or you'd like help choosing a lower-downtime laser

Talk to us first if…

  • –You're pregnant or breastfeeding
  • –You have an active cold sore, skin infection or open wound in the treatment area
  • –You've recently taken isotretinoin (Accutane) — ask your provider about timing
  • –Your main concern is loose or sagging skin — skin tightening with Everesse or Radiant Lift may fit better

Laser Skin Revitalization FAQs

Is laser skin revitalization FDA-cleared?

Yes. The laser platforms we use — Tetra CO2, CoolPeel, and Motus — hold FDA 510(k) clearance for skin resurfacing, soft-tissue coagulation, and treatment of fine lines, textural irregularities, and pigmented lesions.

What's the difference between Tetra CO2, CoolPeel, and Motus?

Tetra CO2 is our most powerful option for significant sun damage, scarring, and deeper wrinkles (5–7 days downtime). CoolPeel delivers similar CO2 technology with faster, lower downtime. Motus Laser Facial is our gentlest, virtually no-downtime option for regular maintenance. We'll help you choose at your consultation.

How much downtime is there after laser skin revitalization?

Downtime depends on the platform: little to none with Motus, a few days with CoolPeel, and typically 5–7 days of redness and peeling with Tetra CO2's standard fractional settings.

Is laser skin revitalization safe for darker skin?

We assess your Fitzpatrick skin type and adjust settings accordingly at your consultation — darker skin tones require more conservative settings to minimize pigment-change risk.

How many laser skin revitalization sessions do I need?

Many clients do 1–3 sessions spaced 4–6 weeks apart. The number depends on the laser you choose, how deep your concerns are and your goals. Your provider will recommend a plan at your consultation.

What should I avoid after laser skin revitalization?

Avoid sun exposure and wear sunscreen daily while your skin heals. Don't pick or peel flaking skin. Skip retinoids, exfoliants and harsh products until your provider says it's okay. Use the gentle cleanser and moisturizer they recommend.

How long do laser skin revitalization results last?

Results can be long-lasting. Collagen keeps rebuilding for 3–6 months after treatment. Your skin still ages and sees sun, though, so daily SPF and occasional maintenance sessions help you keep your results. Results vary from person to person.
