Dissolve HA Filler in Ocala, FL

Dissolving HA filler is a treatment that uses hyaluronidase, an enzyme injected into hyaluronic-acid filler, to break it down. It suits anyone with overfilled, lumpy or migrated filler, a bluish Tyndall effect, or a simple change of heart. Visits take 15–30 minutes with minimal downtime, and filler dissolves within hours to about a day.

What can dissolving HA filler fix?

Dissolving HA filler corrects results you're not happy with — overfilled or lumpy areas, filler that has migrated, a bluish Tyndall effect, or filler you'd simply like removed.

Overfilled or Lumpy Results

Smoothing filler that's migrated or over-corrected.

Migrated Filler

Correcting product that has shifted from its original placement.

Tyndall Effect

A bluish tint from filler placed too superficially.

Change of Mind

Simply want previous filler removed.

How does dissolving HA filler work?

Hyaluronidase is an enzyme that breaks down hyaluronic acid. Your provider dilutes it in saline and injects it directly into the filler you want reduced or removed. Less cross-linked filler can dissolve in 1–2 hours, while denser, longer-lasting filler may take up to 24 hours. Hyaluronidase only works on hyaluronic acid, so it doesn't affect your natural collagen or non-HA fillers such as Radiesse or Sculptra.

Is dissolving HA filler right for me?

A good fit if…

  • ✓Your hyaluronic-acid filler looks overfilled, lumpy or uneven
  • ✓Filler has migrated from where it was placed
  • ✓You see a bluish tint (Tyndall effect) from filler placed too superficially
  • ✓You'd like to start fresh before new filler

Talk to us first if…

  • –Your filler isn't hyaluronic acid (for example Radiesse or Sculptra) — hyaluronidase won't dissolve it
  • –You've had an allergic reaction to hyaluronidase, or a severe reaction to bee or wasp stings
  • –You have an active skin infection in the treatment area
  • –You're pregnant or breastfeeding

Dissolve HA Filler FAQs

Does dissolving filler hurt?

It's injected similarly to filler itself — most clients feel mild pressure and tolerate it well.

How fast does hyaluronidase dissolve filler?

Less cross-linked fillers can dissolve in as little as 1–2 hours; denser, longer-lasting fillers may take up to 24 hours for full effect.

Will dissolving filler affect my natural tissue?

No — hyaluronidase only breaks down hyaluronic acid. It has no effect on your natural collagen or other filler types like Radiesse or Sculptra.

How many sessions does it take to dissolve filler?

Many people need only one session. Larger amounts of filler or denser products sometimes need a follow-up treatment, and your provider will check your results and recommend whether another session is needed.

How soon can I get new filler after dissolving?

Your provider will usually suggest waiting until any swelling has settled and the old filler has fully dissolved before placing new filler. They'll confirm the right timing for you at your follow-up visit.

What should I avoid after dissolving filler?

For about 24 hours, avoid strenuous exercise and pressing on or massaging the treated area unless your provider tells you to. Mild swelling or redness is normal and usually fades within a few days.
