What does skin tightening treat?

Skin tightening treats loose skin on the face and neck, a softening jawline and the delicate under-eye area. It can also firm body areas such as the knees and abdomen.

How does Everesse skin tightening work?

Everesse (VOLNEWMER), FDA-cleared in April 2024, is a radiofrequency device, not a laser. It sends RF energy into the deeper layers of your skin while continuous cooling protects the surface and keeps you comfortable. The heat makes existing collagen fibers contract, which is why you see some tightening right after treatment. It also signals your skin to build new collagen, so firmness keeps improving over the following months.

Is skin tightening right for me?

A good fit if…

  • ✓You're noticing early loosening on your face, neck or jawline
  • ✓You'd like gentle firming around your eyes
  • ✓You want to firm body areas such as the knees or abdomen
  • ✓You want a no-needle, no-downtime treatment

Talk to us first if…

  • –You have a pacemaker or another implanted electronic device
  • –You have significant metal implants in the treatment area
  • –You're pregnant
  • –Your main concern is sun damage, fine lines or acne scars on the surface — laser skin revitalization is usually the better fit

Skin Tightening FAQs

Is Everesse FDA-cleared?

Yes — Everesse (VOLNEWMER) received FDA 510(k) clearance in April 2024 for non-invasive skin tightening.

Does Everesse skin tightening hurt?

No — it's designed to be pain-free, with continuous cooling for comfort throughout the treatment.

Who shouldn't get RF skin tightening?

Those with pacemakers or other implanted electronic devices, significant metal implants in the treatment area, or who are pregnant should avoid RF treatments — we screen for this at consultation.

How many skin tightening sessions do I need?

Most treatment plans call for 2 Everesse sessions about 6 months apart. Your provider will confirm the right plan for your treatment areas and goals at your consultation.

How long do skin tightening results last?

You'll see some tightening right away, and results keep building over about 6 months as new collagen forms. How long they last depends on your age, skin and lifestyle, since your skin keeps aging. Your provider can suggest maintenance. Results vary.

Skin tightening vs. Radiant Lift: what's the difference?

Both are non-invasive radiofrequency treatments with no downtime. Everesse skin tightening treats the face, neck and body areas like the knees and abdomen, while Radiant Lift focuses on the face and neck. We'll help you choose based on your goals and budget.
