How do custom facials and chemical peels work?

Your provider starts with a skin analysis, then cleanses, exfoliates and performs extractions where needed. An enzyme or chemical peel loosens the bonds between dull, dead surface cells so they shed and reveal fresher skin underneath, which helps even tone and smooth texture. Medical-grade serums matched to your concerns finish the treatment. Peel depth is adjusted to your goals and schedule, from light peels with little flaking to medium-depth peels.

Is a custom facial or chemical peel right for me?

A good fit if…

  • ✓Your skin looks dull, uneven or tired
  • ✓You deal with clogged pores, congestion or breakouts
  • ✓You have sun spots, pigmentation or rough texture you want to soften
  • ✓You want a results-focused facial built around your skin, not a one-size-fits-all routine

Talk to us first if…

  • –You have an active skin infection, open wounds or a cold sore flare in the area
  • –You've recently used isotretinoin (Accutane) — peels are usually postponed
  • –You're sunburned or have a vacation in the sun planned right after a peel
  • –Your main concern is deeper wrinkles or acne scarring — microneedling or laser skin revitalization may be the better fit

Facial & Chemical Peel FAQs

What's included in a custom facial?

Cleansing, exfoliation, extractions if needed, and a mask, peel, or serum blend selected specifically for your skin that day — plus optional add-ons like LED, dermaplaning, or radio frequency.

Will I peel after a chemical peel?

Light peels cause little to no visible flaking. Medium-depth peels can cause visible peeling for 5–7 days — we'll match the depth to your schedule and goals.

How often should I get a facial or chemical peel?

For active concerns, a series of 4–6 sessions every 2–4 weeks is common, with monthly visits for maintenance.

What should I avoid after a chemical peel?

Avoid picking or peeling flaking skin, direct sun, exfoliants and retinoids until your skin has healed — usually about a week after a medium-depth peel. Use a gentle cleanser, moisturizer and daily broad-spectrum sunscreen.

Are chemical peels safe for darker skin?

Yes, when the peel type and depth are chosen carefully. Darker skin tones can be more prone to pigment changes, so your provider will select a peel suited to your skin and goals and may suggest a gentler approach.

What's the difference between a custom facial and a mini facial?

A custom facial is a 60-minute corrective treatment with extractions, peels and targeted serums for specific concerns. A Mini Facial (Express Glo) is a 30-minute refresh — cleansing, light exfoliation and hydration — ideal for maintenance between deeper treatments.
