Our Services

Lash Lift & Tint

Curls and tints your natural lashes for a mascara-free, wide-eyed look lasting 6–8 weeks.

Lash Extensions

Individual synthetic or silk lashes applied to your natural lashes for fuller, longer lashes.

Brow Lamination

Sets brow hairs into a fuller, more uniform shape for a bold, brushed-up look.

Brow Shaping & Tint

Waxing or threading plus tint to define and balance your natural brow shape.

Lash Fills

Maintenance appointments to keep extensions full between full sets.

Lash & Brow Combo

Pair any lash and brow service in one visit for a complete refresh.

Aftercare

  • Keep lashes/brows dry for 24 hours after your service.
  • Avoid oil-based makeup removers and skincare near the treated area.
  • Brush lash extensions gently with a spoolie each morning.
  • Schedule fills every 2–3 weeks to keep extensions full.

FAQ

Lash lift vs. lash extensions — what's the difference?

A lash lift curls and tints your natural lashes for a low-maintenance look lasting 6-8 weeks. Extensions add individual fibers for longer, fuller lashes that need periodic fills.

How long does brow lamination last?

Typically 6-8 weeks.

Is there any downtime?

None — just keep lashes/brows dry for the first 24 hours.
