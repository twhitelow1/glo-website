Common Questions

Is SkinPen FDA-cleared?
Yes — SkinPen was the first microneedling device to earn FDA clearance, in 2018, for facial acne scars, with an expanded clearance in 2021 for neck wrinkles.
Is microneedling safe for my skin tone?
Yes. Because it works mechanically rather than with heat or light, microneedling is considered safe across all Fitzpatrick skin types.
How many sessions will I need?
Most clients see the best results from a series of 3 or more sessions, spaced about 4 weeks apart.
