Common Questions

Does it hurt?
Most clients feel mild pressure; topical numbing cream keeps discomfort minimal.
Is there downtime?
Most return to normal activity same-day; mild swelling or bruising can last a few days.
Can filler be dissolved if I change my mind?
Yes — hyaluronic-acid filler can be dissolved. Ask about our Dissolve HA Dermal Filler service.
