Visiting Ocala? Here's What to Know

Which providers see clients in Ocala?

McKenzie McCalla, Sundee Bass, Baily Bellamy and Mariah Young see clients at our Ocala location. Sundee Bass and Baily Bellamy also see clients in Palatka. You can book any of them online for Ocala, or call 352-559-8034 and we’ll match you.

Do I need a consultation before my first treatment?

Yes — every new client starts with a consultation so we can build a treatment plan personalized to your goals and health history.

Can I get the same treatments at both locations?

Yes — both our Palatka and Ocala locations offer the full GLO treatment menu. Availability of specific devices may vary by location, so ask at booking if you have a specific technology in mind.

How do I book an appointment at the Ocala location?

Use the "Book Your Ocala Consultation" button above, or call us at 352-559-8034.

Where is GLO’s Ocala location?

GLO’s Ocala med spa is at 1925 SW 18th Ct, Unit 109, Ocala, FL 34471. Tap “Get Directions” above for turn-by-turn directions from Google Maps.

What are the Ocala location’s hours?

Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Friday 9am–5pm, Wednesday 9am–6pm, and Saturday and Sunday by appointment. Call 352-559-8034 for same-week availability.
