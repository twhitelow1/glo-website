Location FAQs

Where are GLO’s locations?

GLO Aesthetics + Wellness Lounge has two Florida med spas: 1925 SW 18th Ct, Unit 109, Ocala, FL 34471 (our flagship), and 210 St Johns Ave, Palatka, FL 32177.

Do both locations offer the same treatments?

Both locations offer the full GLO treatment menu. Availability of specific devices may vary by location, so ask when you book if you have a particular technology in mind.

How do I book at a specific location?

Use the “Book in Ocala” or “Book in Palatka” button above to see that location’s real-time availability, or call 352-559-8034.

Is there one phone number for both locations?
